Smart Middle Click Autoscroll

Middle-click scrollable content, then move the pointer to control speed. Move back to the anchor to pause. Click again or press Esc to stop.

Links, tabs, and controls keep their normal middle-click actions. Holding a keyboard modifier also preserves the native click.

Set up

Enable the app in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. This lets it identify controls and generate scrolling across apps. It processes input locally without saving page text or keystrokes.

Menu bar controls

  • Enable or pause autoscroll.
  • Adjust scrolling speed and horizontal scrolling.
  • Keep native middle click in apps that need their own middle-button gestures.

Closing Settings leaves the app running in the menu bar. Use Quit to stop it.

If scrolling does not start

Some apps do not expose enough Accessibility information. Smart Middle Click Autoscroll preserves the native click when it cannot identify a suitable target.

Check Diagnostics in the menu bar for the last routing decision, or retry the input listener. After replacing or rebuilding the app, macOS may require removing and re-adding its Accessibility entry.

If you use a mouse remapper, it must deliver a middle click to the app. Avoid running multiple autoscroll utilities at once.
